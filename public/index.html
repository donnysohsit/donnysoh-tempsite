About

I work at the intersection of applied artificial intelligence and software engineering, with a focus on turning research ideas into systems people can actually use. My day-to-day spans machine learning experimentation, data pipelines, and the tooling that makes results legible to other people.

I care about clear explanations as much as working code. A good deal of my work involves teaching and mentoring — breaking down sequence models, anomaly detection and practical AI engineering for people meeting them for the first time.
